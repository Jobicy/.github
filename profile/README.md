## About Jobicy

[Jobicy](https://jobicy.com) is a remote work and career intelligence platform helping people discover opportunities, understand career paths, research companies, compare salaries, and make better career decisions.

We also build public APIs, datasets, developer resources, integrations, and tools that make structured remote-work data easier to access and use.

Our goal is simple: make high-quality career and employment data more useful for people, developers, researchers, AI systems, and the broader open web.

## What we build

### Remote Jobs

Jobicy aggregates and publishes remote opportunities across engineering, design, marketing, sales, customer success, finance, operations, product, HR, and many other fields.

→ [Browse remote jobs](https://jobicy.com/jobs)

### Jobicy Remote Jobs API

The Jobicy API provides structured access to remote job listings for applications, research projects, aggregators, dashboards, bots, and other integrations.

Example:

```bash
curl "https://jobicy.com/api/v2/remote-jobs?count=100"
```

Typical data includes job titles, companies, locations, industries, employment types, publication dates, descriptions, and application URLs.

→ [Open the Remote Jobs API](https://jobicy.com/api/v2/remote-jobs)

## Our approach to data

We care about making employment data useful rather than simply making it abundant.

Jobicy focuses on:

**Quality** — reducing irrelevant, misleading, expired, and low-quality listings.

**Structure** — making jobs and career information easier for machines and humans to understand.

**Accessibility** — providing useful public interfaces, feeds, APIs, and datasets.

**Open discovery** — making structured career information accessible to search engines, AI systems, developers, and researchers.

**Remote-first** — maintaining a strong focus on distributed and location-flexible work.
